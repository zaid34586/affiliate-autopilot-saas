# The Comment-to-DM Funnel: Turning Reel Comments Into Sales

A comment-to-DM funnel does one thing: it converts **public engagement** into **private conversation** — and conversations convert to sales. This guide covers the mechanics, the message flow, and the guardrails that keep you off Instagram's spam radar.

## What is a comment-to-DM funnel?

You post a Reel. The CTA asks viewers to comment a keyword ("GUIDE", "PRICE", "LINK"). When someone comments, they automatically receive a DM with what they asked for — plus your affiliate link or offer.

Why it works:

- **Commenting is a commitment.** People who comment have self-qualified.
- **DMs have 3–5× the open rate of email.** Instagram DMs are read within minutes.
- **Public comments boost distribution.** Each comment signals the algorithm, so the funnel feeds its own reach.

## The message flow that converts

Most funnels fail because the DM immediately dumps a link. The converting flow has four messages:

### Message 1: Deliver the promise (instant)

> "Hey! Here's the guide you asked for: [link] — it covers X, Y, Z. Want me to send the 2-minute summary too?"

Deliver what they asked for, immediately. Then open a loop with a question.

### Message 2: Qualify (within the hour)

> "Quick question — are you starting from scratch, or do you already have an audience?"

Their answer tells you which offer to pitch. Two branches, two scripts.

### Message 3: Recommend with reasoning

> "Based on that, [product] is the better fit — here's why: [one specific reason tied to their answer]. It's $49 and there's a 30-day refund."

Specificity beats hype. One reason, tied to their answer, beats five generic benefits.

### Message 4: Follow-up on silence (24–72 hours)

> "Hey — still deciding? The most common hesitation is [objection]. Happy to answer anything."

80% of sales come from message 4. Most creators never send it.

## Triggers that work

- **Keyword triggers** on comments ("GUIDE", "PRICE", "CHECKLIST") — highest intent.
- **Reply triggers** when someone replies to your Story.
- **New-follower triggers** with a welcome resource (use sparingly — Instagram throttles aggressive welcome DMs).

## The anti-spam rules (do not skip these)

Instagram actively restricts accounts that behave like spam bots. Hard limits:

1. **Daily caps.** Keep DMs under 20–30 per day per account. Accounts sending 100+ daily get flagged within weeks.
2. **Never DM someone who didn't initiate.** Cold DMs are the fastest path to a ban. Comment-triggered = they initiated.
3. **Cooldowns.** One DM per person per 24 hours, maximum. If they don't reply, wait before following up.
4. **Blocklist respected.** If someone says "stop", they're removed permanently — this is both compliance and basic decency.
5. **Human cadence.** Messages should go out spread through the day, not 50 within one minute. Bots fire in bursts; humans don't.

A good automation platform enforces all five automatically. If yours doesn't, you are the enforcement — set calendar reminders and check daily caps yourself.

## Measuring the funnel

Track these four numbers weekly:

- **Comment conversion:** comments ÷ reel views. Below 0.3% = weak CTA.
- **DM delivery rate:** delivered ÷ triggered. Below 95% = trigger problems.
- **Reply rate:** replies ÷ delivered. Below 50% = Message 1 is too salesy.
- **Sale rate:** purchases ÷ replies. Below 5% = wrong offer or weak Message 3.

Diagnose from the top: fix the weakest stage first, never all four at once.

## The one-sentence version

Ask for a comment, deliver instantly, qualify with one question, recommend with one reason, and follow up once — at human speed, under daily caps. That's the whole funnel. Everything else is optimization.
